01 The idea

A spray for the hour after you sweat, made to be used in the open.

Pola Cooldown is a hypochlorous acid (HOCl) spray for face, body and scalp. Use it after the gym, on a flight or before a meeting. It soothes skin, helps keep it clean and needs no rinse.

HOCl is a molecule the body makes itself to fight bacteria, so it is gentle on sensitive skin.

Water+Salt+Electrolysis

The bottle is the status symbol

HOCl breaks down in light, so the bottle has to be opaque. That becomes the look: playful shapes, marbled caps, key rings and clips.

02 The consumer

20 to 32 year olds whose day keeps going after they sweat.

Persona A

The Gym Regular

  • Trains before work or college.
  • The gym shower is shared, queued or skipped.
  • Goes straight to the office, a class or a date.
  • Notices what's in everyone's gym bag.

Persona B

The Mover

  • Early flights, overnight trains, long humid commutes.
  • Hours without a proper wash.

03 The gap

India already has the molecule, but no brand owns the moment.

Why today's options fall short

  1. 01

    Minimalist is a clinical toner; "post-workout" is hidden in the usage notes.

  2. 02

    Deodorants, wipes and sanitizers mask odour with alcohol and fragrance, which sensitive skin often reacts to.

  3. 03

    Steroid creams come after the damage and are linked to recurring ringworm.

  4. 04

    Tower 28 and Touchland prove design sells hygiene, but neither is in India or made for sweat.

09 Your first move

The first 30 days.

Week 1 · Listen

Gym-bag audit

40 interviews across value gyms, chain gyms, studios and run clubs in Delhi NCR and Bengaluru. Photograph 50 bags.

Week 2 · Fake door

Waitlist and price

Show 3 designs at ₹399 / ₹549 / ₹699 on Instagram. Riya's travel audience tests the mover segment.

Week 3 · Make 200

Seed and watch

200 white-label HOCl bottles from a contract manufacturer. Seed 2 gyms and 1 run club. Count who sprays in the open.

Week 4 · Decide

Go or stop

Ask testers to re-buy at full price. Book a regulatory call. Shortlist a formulator for stability testing.

30%

Keep going if

At least 30% of testers pay ₹549 for a second bottle and people use it in the gym without being asked.

These are targets, not results.
